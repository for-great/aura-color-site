Aura Color — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Aura Color ("the app") is published by Maksym Davydov, sole entrepreneur ("we").

Contact: aura-support@p2u.top (postal address in the Legal notice)

The app does not collect any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

  • Your game progress (aura points, best scores, shop items, daily missions, your party name) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • If you use PARTY mode, the name you type and your score are sent directly over your local network to the other phones in that lobby. They never pass through us or any server. iOS asks for your permission to use the local network first; you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Reminders are optional. If you allow them, they are scheduled on your device by iOS. There is no push server and nothing is sent to us.
  • Clips are optional. If you turn on CLIPS, the game keeps a video of the last seconds of each run on your device (only its own picture: nothing from other apps, no microphone, no camera). A clip leaves your phone only if you share it yourself; Save Video adds it to your Photos after asking your permission.
  • The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone or location, does not read your photos (it can only add a clip you choose to save), and does not track you.
  • We never share or sell any data.

The app can open our TikTok page in your browser or the TikTok app. TikTok's own privacy policy applies there.

If you email us, we get your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and don't keep it longer than that needs.

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we know about you, and to correct or delete it: aura-support@p2u.top. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding ads, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: aura-support@p2u.top
