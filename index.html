Quick help

  • How to play: tap anywhere and keep the white marker on the gauge inside the green zone. Too slow and the clock runs out; too fast and your pencil slips out of the lines.
  • PARTY with friends: all phones must be on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, and Aura Color needs the Local Network permission (Settings → Aura Color → Local Network).
  • Reminders: turn them on or off on the home screen (REMINDERS) or in Settings → Notifications → Aura Color.
  • Progress is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
